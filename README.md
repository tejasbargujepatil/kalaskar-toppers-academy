1. High-Conversion & Trust-Building Features

Interactive Results & Toppers Showcase: Instead of static posters, a searchable, dynamic wall of fame where users can filter toppers by year, exam (NEET/JEE), score, and the engineering/medical colleges they got into.

Dynamic Fee & Scholarship Calculator: A self-service widget where parents input their child's 10th-grade board marks or mock scores to instantly calculate tuition discounts or scholarship eligibility. This captures high-intent leads immediately.

Live "Success Tracker" Counter: A prominent homepage counter showing real-time stats (e.g., "1,200+ MBBS Admissions | 500+ IITians/NITians | 100% Personal Attention").

2. Interactive Academic Engagement

Free Mock Test & Instant Score Analysis: A mini-testing portal where students can take a quick 15-minute diagnostic test for JEE/NEET. Upon completion, the system emails them a detailed graphic analysis of their strengths and weaknesses, prompting a call-to-action to book a counseling session.

360° Virtual Campus & Hostel Tour: High-quality, interactive 360-degree views of the smart classrooms, the 24x7 library, and the hostel facilities. This is crucial for attracting out-of-town students from the wider Ahmednagar and Pune districts.

Faculty Video Snippets: Short, engaging video lectures or concept-explainer clips from Prof. Ganesh Kalaskar and key faculty members built directly into the course pages to showcase teaching quality before enrollment.

3. Parent & Student Friction Reduction

Smart Route Transit Map: Since they offer transportation, an interactive map tool where parents can input their village or landmark near Kashti/Shrigonda to see the nearest academy bus pickup point and timings.

AI Career & Exam Guidance Chatbot: An automated WhatsApp-integrated chatbot that answers 24/7 FAQs about batch timings, admission dates, and hostel availability, seamlessly routing complex queries to the academy's sales team.

Integrated Parent-Student Portal (LMS Preview): A secure login gateway where existing parents can view attendance and OMR test performance, giving prospective parents confidence in the academy's structured accountability.
